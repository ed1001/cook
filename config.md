# Cook — Config

_Read first. The rules every recipe and cook plan must respect._

## Diet rules
- Low-GI carbs only (veg, legumes, oats, fruit, whole grains). No refined sugar.
- No boiled eggs. Other egg styles are fine.
- Every meal must contain meat or fish. No meat-free / vegetarian meals.
- No tofu.
- Nutrient-dense: every main carries ≥2 vegetables plus a legume or other
  fibre source; aim ≥7 g fibre per portion. Lean on umami / fermented flavour
  (miso, kimchi, gochujang) over added fat.
- Bulk-friendly: prefer cheap, big-bag veg (boerenkool, cabbage, celeriac,
  leeks, frozen edamame) over premium ones (cavolo nero, asparagus).

## Sides
- Every cook makes **2 sides** alongside the mains. Sides are vegetable-based
  (legumes allowed), meat-free, and **must freeze** — they're portioned and
  frozen next to the mains, no fridge-only sides.
- Side recipes live in `recipes/side-*.md` and carry a `**Side:** freezer` tag.
- Scale sides so total side portions ≈ total main portions (one side per meal).
- Pick two that contrast: one starchy/creamy base (e.g. mash) + one greens/veg
  braise.

## Batch / freeze philosophy
- Recipes are cooked as big **batches** and portioned into the freezer.
- A cook is one long session (~3–4 hr) covering the chosen recipes at once.
- Sequence by freshness: eat fish and fridge portions first (days 1–3); stews/
  braises/curries freeze well and hold ~3 months.
- No per-portion tracking — judge freezer level by eye.

## Portion sizing
- A "portion" = one meal's protein core (~450–500 kcal at ~1× batch).
- Recipes state their batch yield at 1× (e.g. "6 portions"). Scaling a recipe
  by N multiplies both ingredients and yield by N.
- When planning a cook, the target can be given as batches (e.g. "2× each"),
  total portions, or days of food (~2 portions/day).

## Macros
- Each recipe carries rough per-portion macros as a guide only. No daily targets,
  no logging, no tracking.
