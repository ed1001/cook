# Cook — 2026-09-22

**Recipes:** Green Pork Stew (verde, no tomatillo), Filipino Chicken Adobo, Chicken Paprikash — **1× each**.
**Yield:** ~17 portions (~8–9 days). All three are first-time recipes — this is the taste-test run.

> **Night before:** the adobo marinade has to go in the fridge the evening before. See Mise en place.

---

## Shopping list

### Produce
- Onions — 7
- Garlic — 2 heads (~22 cloves)
- Green bell peppers — 6
- Red bell peppers — 3
- Jalapeños — 2
- Green beans — 400 g
- Coriander — 1 big bunch
- Limes — 3

### Meat
- Pork shoulder — 1.2 kg, cubed
- Chicken **bouten** (hele poten), bone-in skin-on — 1.6 kg *(no bone-in thigh available)*
- Chicken thigh, boneless skinless — 1.4 kg

### Pantry
- Chopped tomatoes — 2 × 400 g
- Black beans — 1 × 400 g tin
- Soy sauce — 150 ml
- Cane or white wine vinegar — 200 ml
- Sweet Hungarian paprika — 50 g (recipe uses ~30 g — don't cut it)
- Hot paprika or cayenne — small jar
- Ground cumin — 1 tbsp worth
- Whole black peppercorns — 1 tbsp worth
- Bay leaves — 6
- Dried oregano
- Chicken stock — 800 ml (cubes fine)
- Oil (and lard if you want it for the paprikash)

### Dairy / ferment
- Full-fat sour cream or crème fraîche — 300 ml
  *(Not cooked into the batch — stirred in per portion at reheat.)*

## Mise en place

**The night before**
1. Mix the adobo marinade: 150 ml soy + 150 ml vinegar (¾ of the 200 ml — 50 ml goes to the verde) + 12 crushed garlic cloves (1 head) + 1 tbsp cracked peppercorns + 6 bay leaves. Add all 1.6 kg bouten — **split each at the joint into drumstick + thigh first**. Fridge overnight.

**Cook day, before any heat**
2. Onions: quarter 2 (verde — they go on the roasting tray, so quarters not dice), slice 2 (adobo), finely dice 3 (paprikash). **Keep the three piles separate — they're cut differently.**
3. Garlic: peel 6 cloves whole (verde, roasted), mince 4 (paprikash). The adobo's 12 are already in the marinade.
4. Peppers: quarter + deseed 6 green (verde), slice 3 red (paprikash). Stem 2 jalapeños (verde).
5. Cube 1.2 kg pork shoulder. Halve 1.4 kg boneless thigh.
6. Trim 400 g green beans (adobo).
7. Chop coriander — stalks and leaves in separate piles (verde uses them at different stages).
8. Measure the paprika out now: 4 tbsp sweet + 1 tsp hot in a bowl. It goes in off the heat and you won't have time to measure then.
9. Oven to 220°C.

## Cook plan

Three pots plus the oven, running in parallel. Total ~2.5 hr.

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Verde veg into the 220°C oven (one tray). Start browning pork in Pot 1. |
| 0:30 | Veg out, blitz the green sauce (vinegar in here). Pork + sauce into Pot 1 → low simmer, 1.5–2 hr. Forget about it. |
| 0:35 | Sear adobo chicken in Pot 2 (2–3 batches, skin down). |

## Method — Green Pork Stew / Verde (~6 portions)

> **No tomatillos** — not stocked at AH XL or Jumbo. Sour comes from vinegar + lime, body from extra roasted green pepper. Tastes greener and rounder, less citric-sharp than true chile verde.

*Shared with other pots: onions, garlic, stock, oil, vinegar. Exclusive to this pot: all the pork, all the green peppers, all the jalapeños, all the black beans, all the coriander, all the limes, all the cumin.*

1. Oven at 220°C. On one tray: **6 green bell peppers (all)**, **2 jalapeños (all)**, **2 onions, quartered (2 of the 7)**, **6 garlic cloves, peeled (6 of the 22)**. Oil, salt. Roast 25–30 min until blackening at the edges.
2. Meanwhile brown **1.2 kg pork shoulder (all the pork)** in batches in Pot 1 — don't crowd it, two or three batches. Set aside.
3. Blitz the roasted veg with **all the coriander stalks**, **1 tbsp ground cumin (all)** and **50 ml vinegar (¼ of the 200 ml — the rest is the adobo's)** to a coarse green sauce. No tomatillos available locally; the sour comes from the vinegar and the limes instead.
4. Pork back in Pot 1 with the sauce, **300 ml stock (of the 800 ml)** and a big pinch dried oregano. Low simmer 1.5–2 hr until the pork shreds under a fork.
5. Stir in **1 × 400 g black beans, drained (all)**, the juice of **3 limes (all)** and **all the coriander leaves**. Simmer 10 min. Season hard — it needs more salt than you think.
6. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Method — Filipino Chicken Adobo (~5 portions)

> **Bouten, not thigh** — more bone per kilo, so yield drops to ~5. Split at the joint before marinating; one piece is roughly one portion.

*Shared: onions, oil, vinegar. Exclusive to this pot: all the bouten, all the soy, all the green beans, all the bay, all the peppercorns, and the 12 garlic cloves already sitting in the marinade.*

1. Lift **all 1.6 kg marinated bouten** out of the marinade — **keep every drop of the marinade**.
2. Sear skin-side down in a hot dry Pot 2 until browned, 2–3 batches. Bouten are round — brown the meatiest face, don't chase full coverage. Don't rush it — the rendered fat is the cooking fat. Set chicken aside.
3. Soften **2 sliced onions (2 of the 7)** in the rendered fat.
4. Chicken back in with **all the marinade** + **200 ml water**. Simmer uncovered **45–55 min** (bouten need longer than thigh), turning once, until the sauce is glossy and reduced by half. **Leave it alone for the first 10 min** — the raw vinegar needs to cook off undisturbed.
5. **400 g green beans (all)** in for the last 6–8 min.
6. Taste: sharp and salty, not sweet. Cool, portion bone-in, freeze.

## Method — Chicken Paprikash (~6 portions)

*Shared: onions, garlic, stock, oil. Exclusive to this pot: all the boneless chicken, all the red peppers, all the chopped tomatoes, all the paprika, all the sour cream.*

1. Brown **1.4 kg boneless skinless thigh, halved (all)** in batches in oil or lard in Pot 3. Set aside.
2. Soften **3 finely diced onions (3 of the 7)** + **3 sliced red peppers (all)** slowly in the fat, 10–12 min, until properly sweet. Add **4 minced garlic cloves (4 of the 22)**, 1 min.
3. **Take the pot off the heat.** Stir in **all the measured paprika (4 tbsp sweet + 1 tsp hot)** — paprika turns bitter over direct flame. 30 sec in the residual heat only.
4. Back on low: **2 × 400 g chopped tomatoes (all)** + **400 ml stock (the other ½ of the 800 ml)** + the chicken. Simmer 40–50 min until thick and deep red.
5. Season. **Split the pot here:** ladle out what you're eating in the next two days and stir sour cream into *those* portions off the boil. **The rest freezes plain — no cream.**
6. Cool, portion, freeze.

---

## Freeze sequencing

- **Eat fresh (days 1–3):** paprikash — taste it with the cream before the rest goes in the freezer.
- **Freeze all:** verde and adobo both freeze excellently and deepen with time. The adobo genuinely improves after a week.
- **All three are untested.** Taste each pot at the end of its cook and fix the seasoning while you still can — that's the whole point of doing 1×.
- 300 ml sour cream stays in the fridge. ~50 ml per paprikash portion at reheat, stirred in off the heat — boiling it curdles.
