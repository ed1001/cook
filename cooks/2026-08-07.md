## Mise en place
- Cube 2.4 kg pork; dice 2.4 kg chicken thigh — keep separate boards/bowls.
- Dice 12 onions into 3 piles of 4.
- Mince 32 garlic cloves into 3 piles: 8 / 12 / 12.
- Grate 2 thumbs ginger (korean).
- Slice 500 g mushrooms.
- Stem + chop 400 g kale.
- Dice 2 red bell peppers.
- Slice 8 spring onions.
- Chop coriander (hold until the end).
- Drain: 4 tins cannellini, 4 tins butter beans, 4 tins black beans.
- Halve 2 limes.
- Measure 6 tbsp gochujang + 2 tbsp gochugaru into one dish.
- **First thing:** roast 4 yellow/orange peppers (oven 220°C, ~25 min, until blistered). They cool while you do everything else. Peel, blend smooth with 1 tsp turmeric.
- **2 madame jeanette:** deseed, keep the white membrane, mince fine. Wash hands with soap after — plain water spreads capsaicin. Don't rub your eyes for a couple of hours.

## Cook plan (single session, three pots)
1. **Pork pot first** — longest braise. Brown 2.4 kg pork in batches, soften onions + garlic, paprika, tomatoes + stock + bay. Low simmer 1.5–2 hr. Lid on, walk away.
2. **Ají pot next** (t+20 min). Soften onion/garlic/pepper, ají + cumin + oregano, brown 2.4 kg chicken, tomatoes + butter beans + water. Simmer 30–35 min.
3. **Korean pot** (t+40 min, while ají simmers). Brown 2 kg mince in batches — this is the big-surface job, use the widest pan. Soften onion/garlic/ginger/mushrooms, gochujang, return mince, tomatoes + black beans + soy + water. Simmer 30 min.
4. Finish ají: coriander + lime off heat. Start cooling.
5. Finish korean: spring onions off heat. Start cooling.
6. Back to pork: stir in cannellini + kale, 15 min to wilt + thicken. Season hard (long braises need it). Cool.
7. Portion all three + freeze.

**Pinch point:** browning 2.4 kg pork and 2 kg mince both need dry high heat. Do pork browning first and completely, then reuse that pan for the mince — the fond helps the korean.

## Method — Pork & White Bean Stew (2×)

_Shared across pots: onions (12), garlic (32 cloves), chopped tomatoes (8 tins). Everything else below is used up entirely by this recipe._

1. Brown **2.4 kg pork** (all of it) in batches (don't crowd), set aside. If using chorizo (**200 g, all**), render first + reserve the oil.
2. Soften **4 onions (⅓ of the 12)** + **8 garlic cloves (¼ of the 32)** in the fat until gold; stir in **2 tbsp smoked paprika (all)** 30 sec.
3. Return pork, add **4 × 400 g chopped tomatoes (½ of the 8 tins)** + **~1000 ml stock (all)** + bay. Simmer low 1.5–2 hr until pork tender.
4. Stir in **4 × 400 g cannellini (all 4 tins)** + **400 g kale (all)**, simmer 15 min to wilt + thicken. Season.
5. Cool, portion, freeze. → ~12 portions

## Method — Korean Gochujang Beef & Bean Stew (2×)

_Takes the second ⅓ of the onions and 12 of the 32 garlic cloves. Ginger, mushrooms, spring onions, gochujang, gochugaru, soy and sesame oil are used only here._

1. Brown **2 kg beef mince (all of it)** in batches, set aside.
2. Soften **4 onions (⅓ of the 12)** + **12 garlic cloves (12 of the 32)** + **2 thumbs ginger (all)** + **500 g mushrooms (all)** in **2 tbsp sesame oil (all)**.
3. Stir in **6 tbsp gochujang (all)** + **2 tbsp gochugaru (all)**, cook 1 min. Return mince.
4. Add **2 × 400 g chopped tomatoes (¼ of the 8 tins)** + **4 × 400 g black beans (all 4)** + ~400 ml water + **2 tbsp soy (all)**. Simmer 30 min.
5. Stir through **8 spring onions (all)**. Cool, portion, freeze. → ~12 portions

## Method — Peruvian-style Yellow Chili Chicken (2×)

_Takes the last ⅓ of the onions and the remaining 12 garlic cloves. All the peppers (red, yellow/orange, madame jeanette), cumin, oregano, turmeric, coriander and limes go here._

1. Soften **4 onions (⅓ of the 12)** + **12 garlic cloves (the remaining 12 of 32)** + **2 red peppers (all)** in 2 tbsp oil. Add the roasted-pepper blend (**all 4 yellow/orange peppers** + **1 tsp turmeric, all**) + **2 minced madame jeanette (all)** + **4 tsp cumin (all)** + **2 tsp oregano (all)**. Cook 2–3 min to drive off the water and lose the raw edge.
2. Add **2.4 kg chicken thigh (all of it)**, brown.
3. Add **2 × 400 g chopped tomatoes (the last ¼ of the 8 tins)** + **4 × 400 g butter beans (all 4)** + ~600 ml water. Simmer 30–35 min until sauce thickens.
4. Stir through **all the coriander** + **juice of 2 limes (all)**. Cool, portion, freeze. → ~12 portions
