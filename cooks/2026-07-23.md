# Cook 2026-07-23

**2× each** — Thai red curry chicken, Korean gochujang beef, beef & dark beer stew. ~38 portions → ~2.5 weeks. One ~4-hr session.

## Shopping list

### Meat & fish
- Chicken thigh (boneless, skinless), diced — 2.4 kg
- Lean beef mince (5%) — 2 kg
- Stewing beef, cubed — 3 kg

### Produce
- Onions — 10 (6 sliced for stew, 4 diced for Korean)
- Garlic — 16 cloves
- Ginger — 4 thumbs
- Bell peppers — 4
- Green beans — 400 g
- Spinach — 600 g
- Mushrooms — 500 g
- Spring onions — 8
- Carrots — 8
- Limes — 2

### Pantry
- Thai red curry paste — 8 tbsp (~1 jar)
- Light coconut milk — 2 × 400 ml
- Chickpeas — 4 × 400 g
- Black beans — 4 × 400 g
- Chopped tomatoes — 2 × 400 g
- Gochujang — 6 tbsp
- Gochugaru (chili flakes) — 2 tbsp
- Fish sauce — 4 tbsp
- Soy / tamari — 2 tbsp
- Sesame oil — 2 tbsp
- Cooking oil — ~4 tbsp
- Dark beer (dubbel / stout / bruin) — 2 bottles (330–500 ml)
- Bay leaves, salt, pepper
- Optional (stew): mustard 2 tbsp, thyme, splash of vinegar

### Dairy & ferment
- Kimchi — 1 jar (serve on the Korean beef; natural pairing)

## Mise en place
_Prep all before any pot goes on — one big chopping session._
- Onions: slice 6, dice 4.
- Mince 16 garlic cloves; grate 4 thumbs ginger.
- Slice 4 bell peppers; trim 400 g green beans; slice 500 g mushrooms; slice 8 spring onions.
- Chunk 8 carrots. Wash 600 g spinach. Halve 2 limes.
- Open + drain: 4 × chickpeas, 4 × black beans. Open: 2 × coconut milk, 2 × chopped tomatoes.
- Measure pastes: 8 tbsp red curry paste; 6 tbsp gochujang + 2 tbsp gochugaru.
- Cube/pat dry the 3 kg stewing beef (dry beef browns better).

## Cook plan (~4 hr, one session)
Largest pots — brown all meat in batches (crowded pan steams, won't brown).
1. **Beer stew first** (longest simmer): brown the 3 kg beef, build the pot, set to a low 2–2.5 hr simmer.
2. **Korean beef** while the stew simmers: ~40 min active.
3. **Thai curry** in parallel on a third burner: ~40 min active.
4. Cool everything, portion, freeze.

## Beef & dark beer stew (2× → ~14 portions)
1. Brown **3 kg cubed beef** in batches in a little oil, don't crowd; set aside.
2. Soften **6 sliced onions** slowly in the fat until deep gold.
3. Return beef; add **2 bottles dark beer** + **8 chunked carrots** + **bay** + water to nearly cover. (Optional: **2 tbsp mustard**, thyme, splash vinegar.)
4. Simmer low **2–2.5 hr** until beef is pull-apart tender. Reduce to thicken.
5. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Korean gochujang beef & bean stew (2× → 12 portions)
1. Brown **2 kg beef mince**, set aside. Soften **4 diced onions + 12 garlic cloves + 2 thumbs ginger + 500 g mushrooms** in **2 tbsp sesame oil**.
2. Stir in **6 tbsp gochujang + 2 tbsp gochugaru**, cook 1 min. Return mince.
3. Add **2 × 400 g chopped tomatoes + 4 × 400 g black beans + ~400 ml water + 2 tbsp soy**. Simmer **30 min**.
4. Stir through **8 sliced spring onions**. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Thai red curry chicken (2× → 12 portions)
1. Soften **4 garlic cloves + 2 thumbs ginger** in **2 tbsp oil**. Add **8 tbsp red curry paste**, fry 1 min until fragrant.
2. Add **2.4 kg diced chicken thigh**, brown.
3. Add **2 × 400 ml coconut milk + 4 × 400 g chickpeas + 4 sliced peppers + 400 g green beans + ~400 ml water**. Simmer **25–30 min**.
4. Stir through **600 g spinach** to wilt; add **4 tbsp fish sauce + juice of 2 limes**. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Freeze sequencing
- No fish this cook → nothing to rush. All three freeze well (~3 months); flavour deepens on curry + stew.
- Eat the first few days from the fridge, freeze the rest.
- Each meal: one protein portion + carb side (rice / potato) + veg or salad + kimchi (esp. on the Korean beef) + olive oil / avocado.
