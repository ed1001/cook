# Cook — 2026-10-08

**Mains:** Bulgogi Beef & Cabbage, Miso-Ginger Pork Shoulder & Daikon, Chicken Saag & Chickpeas — **1.5× each**.
**Sides:** Celeriac & White Bean Mash, Braised Boerenkool, Leek & Chickpeas — **~2.25× each** (rounded to whole tins/bags; celeriac trimmed to 3).
**Yield:** ~27 main portions + ~26 side portions (~13–14 days). All five recipes are first-time — taste every pot before it's portioned.

> **Optional, night before:** cut + marinate the saag chicken (last item in Prep) for deeper flavour. Otherwise it marinates while the pork and beef cook.

## Mise en place

Veg first, meat last — one board, no cross-contamination. Every line is one job; tick it off.

### Veg

#### Celeriac — 3
- Peel + cube all 3, hold in a bowl of water *(mash — biggest job, ~15 min, start here)*

#### Onions — 8
- Slice 3 *(bulgogi)*
- Finely dice 5 *(saag)*

#### Garlic — ~64 cloves
- Peel all of it
- Grate 24 *(bulgogi, saag)*
- Crush 12 *(pork)*
- Leave 14 whole *(mash)*
- Slice 14 *(boerenkool)*

#### Ginger — 200 g
- Grate 160 g *(bulgogi, saag, pork)*
- Slice 40 g *(pork)*

#### Carrots — 10
- 5 chunky *(pork)*
- 5 matchsticks *(bulgogi)*

#### Cabbage — 1
- Quarter, core, shred all *(bulgogi)*

#### Daikon — 900 g
- Peel, thick half-moons, all *(pork)*

#### Mushrooms — 450 g
- Halve all *(pork)*

#### Leeks — 7
- Wash + slice all *(boerenkool)*

#### Spring onions — 18
- Slice all 18 *(bulgogi, pork)*

#### Green chillies — 3
- Slit all *(saag)*

### Tins, sauces, measuring
- **Chickpeas** — drain 10 tins into three bowls: 2 pork / 3 saag / 5 boerenkool
- **Cannellini** — drain all 5 tins *(mash)*
- **Bulgogi sauce jug** — grate **2 pears (all)** straight into **9 tbsp soy (9 of the 15)** so they don't brown; **12 grated garlic (½ of the 24)**, **40 g grated ginger (¼ of the 160 g)**, **3 tbsp sesame oil (all)**, lots of black pepper
- **Sesame seeds** — toast all 3 tbsp in a dry pan, 2 min, set aside *(bulgogi)*
- **Miso** — measure 6 tbsp into a bowl *(pork)*

Spinach + edamame stay in the freezer until they go in.

### Meat — last
- **Chicken** — cut 1.8 kg thigh (all) into bite-size chunks. Mix with **225 g yoghurt (of the 500 g)** + **1.5 tbsp garam masala (½ of the 3 tbsp)** + **1.5 tsp turmeric (all)** + salt. Fridge until 1:05.
- **Pork** — check the butcher's cubes are ~4 cm; cut down any big ones. Fridge until 0:00.
- **Beef mince** — stays in the fridge until 0:25.
- Wash the board + knife.

## Cook plan

Three big pots (A, B, C), with B and C reused for the sides. ~2 hr 45 min of cooking, then portioning.

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Pot A:** brown pork in batches (~25 min). |
| 0:25 | **Pot A:** garlic + sliced ginger, soy, water → simmer lid ajar, 1 hr. **Pot B:** start browning mince in batches (~20 min). |
| 0:45 | **Pot B:** onions + carrot matchsticks 5 min → mince back + bulgogi sauce → cabbage in handfuls + water, lid on 20 min. **Pot C:** cumin seeds + onions for saag, low + slow 15 min. |
| 1:05 | **Pot C:** garlic/ginger/chilli, spices, tomatoes 5 min, chicken + marinade in 5 min, then frozen spinach + water. Lid on, 30 min. |
| 1:10 | **Pot B:** edamame in, 5–10 min. |
| 1:20 | **Pot B done.** Spring onions + sesame seeds, taste, decant into containers/trays to cool. **Rinse Pot B.** |
| 1:25 | **Pot A:** daikon, carrots, mushrooms in → 45–60 min. **Pot B:** celeriac + whole garlic in salted water, 20 min. |
| 1:40 | **Pot C:** chickpeas in, lid off 5–10 min. Lemon, season. |
| 1:45 | **Pot B:** cannellini in for 3 min → drain → steam-dry 5 min. |
| 1:50 | **Pot C done.** Decant to cool. **Rinse Pot C.** Blitz the mash (in Pot B or a bowl). |
| 2:00 | **Pot C:** leeks 10 min → garlic → boerenkool in handfuls + stock, lid on 20 min. |
| 2:25 | **Pot A:** miso **off the boil**, chickpeas, grated ginger, vinegar, spring onions. 5 min warm. Done. |
| 2:30 | **Pot C:** chickpeas in, uncovered 5 min. Season. Done. |

## Method — Bulgogi Beef & Cabbage (~9 portions)

*Shared with other pots: onions, garlic, ginger, carrots, spring onions, soy. Exclusive to this pot: all the beef, all the cabbage, all the pears, all the edamame, all the sesame oil + seeds.*

1. Brown **1.8 kg beef mince (all)** hard in Pot B in 3 batches — colour, not grey. Set aside.
2. Splash of oil, **3 sliced onions (3 of the 8)** + **5 carrots in matchsticks (all the matchsticks)**, 5 min.
3. Mince back, **all the bulgogi sauce from the jug**, bubble 2 min.
4. **All the shredded cabbage** in by the handful + **300 ml water**. Lid on, 15–20 min until soft but with some bite — it softens more on reheat.
5. **600 g edamame (all)** straight from frozen, 5–10 min.
6. **9 spring onions (½ of the 18)** + **all the toasted sesame seeds**. Taste — savoury-sweet. Flat → splash more soy.
7. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Method — Miso-Ginger Pork Shoulder & Daikon (~9 portions)

*Shared: garlic, ginger, carrots, chickpeas, spring onions, soy. Exclusive to this pot: all the pork, all the miso, all the daikon, all the mushrooms, all the rice vinegar.*

1. Brown **1.8 kg pork shoulder (all)** in Pot A in batches — don't crowd. Set aside.
2. **12 crushed garlic (all the crushed)** + **40 g sliced ginger (all the sliced)**, 1 min. Pork back, **6 tbsp soy (the other 6 of the 15)** + **~1.2 L water** to just cover. Simmer lid ajar, 1 hr.
3. **900 g daikon (all)**, **5 chunky carrots (all the chunky)**, **450 g halved mushrooms (all)**. Simmer 45–60 min until the pork is spoon-tender and the daikon translucent.
4. **Heat off.** Loosen **6 tbsp miso (all)** with a ladle of liquid, stir in — **never boil after this**. **2 tins chickpeas (2 of the 10)**, **40 g grated ginger (¼ of the 160 g)**, **3 tbsp rice vinegar (all)**. Warm 5 min on lowest heat.
5. **9 spring onions (the other ½)**. Taste — needs salt? Add soy, not salt.
6. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Method — Chicken Saag & Chickpeas (~9 portions)

*Shared: onions, garlic, ginger, chickpeas, lemons, garam masala. Exclusive to this pot: all the chicken, all the spinach, all the tomatoes, all the chillies, all the cumin seeds, turmeric, coriander.*

1. **3 tbsp oil/ghee** hot in Pot C. **1 tbsp cumin seeds (all)** 30 sec until they crackle.
2. **5 diced onions (5 of the 8)**, low and slow 15 min until deep gold. Don't rush — this is the flavour.
3. **12 grated garlic (the other ½ of the 24)**, **80 g grated ginger (½ of the 160 g)**, **3 slit chillies (all)**, 2 min. **1.5 tsp ground coriander (all)** + **1.5 tbsp garam masala (the other ½)**, 30 sec.
4. **2 × 400 g chopped tomatoes (all)**, cook down 5 min.
5. **All the marinated chicken + marinade**, stir 5 min until opaque. **1.5 kg frozen spinach (all)** + **450 ml water**. Lid on, 30 min, stir as the blocks break up.
6. **3 tins chickpeas (3 of the 10)**, lid off 5–10 min to thicken. Juice of **2 lemons (2 of the 4)**. Season hard.
7. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Method — Side: Celeriac & White Bean Mash (~12 side portions)

*Shared: garlic, lemons, olive oil. Exclusive: all the celeriac, all the cannellini, the nutmeg.*

1. In rinsed Pot B: **all 3 cubed celeriac** + **14 whole garlic (all the whole)**, salted water to cover. Simmer ~20 min until very soft.
2. **5 tins cannellini (all)** in for the last 3 min.
3. Drain **well**, let steam-dry 5 min. Wet mash freezes icy.
4. Blitz/mash with **100 ml olive oil (of the 170 ml)**, juice of **2 lemons (the other 2 of the 4)**, good grating of nutmeg, salt, pepper. Keep it thick — it loosens on reheat.
5. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Method — Side: Braised Boerenkool, Leek & Chickpeas (~14 side portions)

*Shared: garlic, chickpeas, olive oil. Exclusive: all the boerenkool, all the leeks, all the stock. Kept neutral — no lemon, no chilli — so it sits under any of the three mains.*

1. In rinsed Pot C: **70 ml olive oil (the rest of the 170 ml)**, **7 sliced leeks (all)** + pinch of salt, 10 min.
2. **14 sliced garlic (all the sliced)**, 1 min.
3. **5 bags boerenkool (all)** in handfuls as it wilts + **450 ml stock (all)**. Lid on, 20 min — it's tougher than cavolo nero, give it time.
4. **5 tins chickpeas (the last 5 of the 10)**, uncovered 5 min to drive off liquid. Season.
5. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Freeze sequencing

- **Everything freezes.** Keep ~3 days in the fridge (1 of each main + matching sides per day), freeze the rest.
- **Pairings:**
  - **Saag + celeriac mash.** The mash stands in for rice. 9 saag → 9 of the ~12 mash.
  - **Bulgogi beef + boerenkool.** Plain garlicky greens under the savoury-sweet beef. 9 bulgogi → 9 of the ~14 boerenkool.
  - **Miso pork + whatever's left**: ~3 mash, ~5 boerenkool. The mash soaks up the braise best.
- Label containers. Celeriac mash and saag look alike frozen.
- **All five are untested.** Taste each pot before portioning and fix seasoning then — that's when you still can.
- Rest of the yoghurt stays in the fridge for spooning on the saag at serving.
