# Cook — 2026-11-12

**Mains:** Jerk Pork, Black-Eyed Beans & Squash, Moroccan Chicken, Lentil & Chickpea Stew, Greek Beef Stifado with Butter Beans & Spinach — **1.5× each**.
**Side:** Lemony Braised Savoy Cabbage, Leek & Peas — **4×** (one side this time; scaled to roughly match the mains, cooked in two pots).
**Yield:** ~27 main portions + ~24 side portions (~13 days). All four recipes are first-time. Taste every pot before you portion it.

> **Optional, night before:** make the jerk paste and marinate the pork overnight (first item in Tins and last item in Meat). It's noticeably better. Otherwise it marinates while the beef browns.

## Mise en place

Veg first, meat last. One board, no cross-contamination. Every line is one job; tick it off.

### Veg

#### Butternut squash — 1 large
- Peel, deseed, 3 cm chunks, all *(jerk)*. The slowest veg job, so do it first.

#### Onions — 12
- Dice 6 *(jerk, moroccan)*
- Thick wedges 6 *(stifado)*

#### Garlic — ~43 cloves
- Peel all of it
- Roughly chop 9 *(jerk paste, goes in the blender)*
- Mince 9 *(moroccan)*
- Slice 25 *(stifado, side)*

#### Ginger — 75 g
- Roughly chop 45 g *(jerk paste)*
- Grate 30 g *(moroccan)*

#### Spring onions — 9
- Slice all; whites and greens in two separate piles *(jerk)*

#### Madame jeanette — 2
- Gloves on. Deseed + roughly chop both *(jerk paste)*

#### Red peppers — 3
- Chunk all *(jerk)*

#### Carrots — 6
- Thick coins, all *(moroccan)*

#### Courgettes — 3
- Chunk all *(moroccan)*

#### Leeks — 12
- Halve lengthways, wash, slice all *(side)*

#### Savoy cabbage — 4
- Core + shred ~1 cm, all *(side)*. It's a mountain; two big bowls.

#### Coriander — 2 bunches
- Chop all *(moroccan)*

#### Citrus — 3 limes, 6 lemons
- Juice all 3 limes into one jug *(jerk)*
- Zest + juice all 6 lemons. Zest in two bowls (2 lemons' worth for the moroccan, 4 for the side); juice the same way.

### Tins, sauces, measuring
- **Jerk paste**: blitz spring onion whites (all), 9 chopped garlic, 45 g chopped ginger, both madame jeanettes, **half the lime juice**, **4.5 tbsp soy (all)**, **1.5 tbsp allspice (¾ of the 2 tbsp)**, **1.5 tbsp thyme (all)**, **1.5 tsp ground cinnamon (½ of the 1 tbsp)**, 1.5 tsp black pepper.
- **Chopped tomatoes**: line up 8 tins in three groups, 2 jerk / 3 moroccan / 3 stifado.
- **Beans**: drain into three bowls: 3 tins black-eyed *(jerk)* / 2 tins chickpeas *(moroccan)* / 3 tins butter beans *(stifado)*.
- **Lentils**: rinse 300 g (all) *(moroccan)*.
- **Moroccan spice bowl**: 1 tbsp cumin, 1 tbsp ground coriander, **1.5 tsp ground cinnamon (the other ½)**, 1.5 tsp turmeric, 1.5 tsp smoked paprika. Harissa 3 tbsp in a ramekin beside it.
- **Stifado spice bowl**: 2 cinnamon sticks, 6 cloves, 5 bay, **1.5 tsp allspice (the rest)**, 1 tbsp oregano. 3 tbsp tomato purée on a saucer. 150 ml vinegar + 225 ml red wine in one jug.
- **Stock**: 800 ml, for the side. Make it now, or at 2:05 when the side starts.

Peas and spinach stay in the freezer until they go in.

### Meat (last)
- **Pork**: check the butcher's cubes are ~4 cm and trim any big fat seams. Toss 1.8 kg (all) in **all the jerk paste**. Fridge until 0:25. *(Better overnight. If so, do this the night before.)*
- **Beef**: check the cubes are ~4 cm; cut down any big ones. Pat dry so it browns.
- **Chicken**: cut 1.8 kg thigh (all) into bite-size chunks. Fridge until 1:00.
- Wash the board + knife.

## Cook plan

Three big pots (A, B, C). B and C get rinsed and reused for the side, which is split into two pots because 4 cabbages won't fit in one. About 3 hours of cooking, then portioning.

| Time | Action |

| 0:00 | **Pot A:** brown beef hard in batches (~25 min). |
| 0:25 | **Pot A:** onion wedges into the fat, 10 min. **Pot B:** start browning jerk pork in batches (~25 min). |
| 0:35 | **Pot A:** garlic + purée 2 min → vinegar/wine 2 min → tomatoes, water, spices, beef back. Lid on, very low **2 hr** (or 160 °C oven). |
| 0:50 | **Pot B:** pork out → onions + peppers, 8 min. |
| 1:00 | **Pot B:** pork + leftover paste back, tomatoes + water. Lid on **1 hr**. **Pot C:** brown chicken in batches (~15 min). |
| 1:15 | **Pot C:** chicken out → onions 8 min → garlic, ginger, spice bowl, harissa. |
| 1:25 | **Pot C:** tomatoes, lentils, carrots, water, lemon zest, chicken back. Lid on, 25 min. |
| 1:50 | **Pot C:** courgettes + chickpeas in, 15 min. |
| 2:00 | **Pot B:** squash + black-eyed beans in, lid on 25–30 min. |
| 2:05 | **Pot C done.** Lemon juice, coriander, season. Decant to cool. **Rinse Pot C.** Side batch 1: leeks. |
| 2:15 | **Pot C:** garlic, then 2 cabbages + stock + zest. Lid on, 15 min. |
| 2:30 | **Pot B done.** Rest of the lime, spring onion greens, season. Decant. **Rinse Pot B.** **Pot C:** peas in, 5 min. |
| 2:35 | **Pot C done (side batch 1).** Lemon, season, decant. **Pot B:** side batch 2, starting with the leeks. |
| 2:35 | **Pot A:** check the beef pulls apart; if not, give it another 15–20 min. Then butter beans + spinach, lid off 15 min. |
| 2:45 | **Pot B:** garlic, then the other 2 cabbages + stock + zest. Lid on, 15 min. |
| 2:50 | **Pot A done.** Fish out the cinnamon, cloves and bay. Season. Decant. |
| 3:00 | **Pot B:** peas in, 5 min. Lemon, season. Done. |
| 3:10 | Everything cooling. Portion + label once it's at room temp. |

## Method — Greek Beef Stifado with Butter Beans & Spinach (~9 portions)

*Shared with other pots: onions, garlic, chopped tomatoes, allspice, olive oil. Exclusive to this pot: all the beef, butter beans, spinach, purée, vinegar, wine, cinnamon sticks, cloves, bay, oregano.*

1. **3 tbsp olive oil** hot in Pot A. Brown **1.8 kg beef (all)** hard in 3–4 batches. You want colour, not grey. Set aside.
2. **6 onions in wedges (all the wedges)** into the fat, 10 min until browned in patches.
3. **9 sliced garlic (9 of the 25 sliced)** + **3 tbsp tomato purée (all)**, 2 min.
4. **All the vinegar + wine jug** (150 ml vinegar, 225 ml wine), scrape the bottom, bubble 2 min.
5. **3 tins chopped tomatoes (3 of the 8)**, **~450 ml water**, **the whole stifado spice bowl**. Beef back in. Lid on, very low **2 hr** until it pulls apart with a spoon.
6. **3 tins butter beans (all)** + **600 g frozen spinach (all)** straight from frozen. Lid off 15 min, stirring as the spinach breaks down, until thick.
7. Fish out the cinnamon sticks, cloves + bay. Season. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Method — Jerk Pork, Black-Eyed Beans & Squash (~9 portions)

*Shared: onions, garlic, ginger, chopped tomatoes, allspice, cinnamon. Exclusive to this pot: all the pork, squash, red peppers, black-eyed beans, spring onions, madame jeanette, limes, soy, thyme.*

1. **3 tbsp oil** hot in Pot B. Brown **all the marinated pork** in batches, scraping paste off the pan bottom as you go. Set aside.
2. **3 diced onions (½ of the 6 diced)** + **3 chunked red peppers (all)** into the fat, 8 min until soft.
3. Pork + any leftover paste back in. **2 tins chopped tomatoes (2 of the 8)** + **~500 ml water**. Lid on, simmer low **1 hr**.
4. **All the squash** + **3 tins black-eyed beans (all)**. Lid on 25–30 min until the pork is tender and the squash is soft but holds its shape.
5. **The rest of the lime juice (the other half)** + **all the spring onion greens**. Taste. Flat → splash more soy; too hot → more lime. Season.
6. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Method — Moroccan Chicken, Lentil & Chickpea Stew (~9 portions)

*Shared: onions, garlic, ginger, chopped tomatoes, lemons, cinnamon. Exclusive to this pot: all the chicken, lentils, chickpeas, carrots, courgettes, harissa, coriander, cumin, turmeric, paprika.*

1. **3 tbsp olive oil** hot in Pot C. Brown **1.8 kg chicken (all)** in batches, set aside.
2. **3 diced onions (the other ½ of the 6 diced)**, 8 min until soft.
3. **9 minced garlic (all the minced)**, **30 g grated ginger (all the grated)**, **the whole Moroccan spice bowl**, 1 min. **3 tbsp harissa (all)**, 30 sec.
4. **3 tins chopped tomatoes (the last 3 of the 8)**, **300 g lentils (all)**, **6 carrots (all)**, **~1.35 L water**, **the moroccan lemon zest (2 lemons' worth)**. Chicken back in. Lid on, simmer 25 min.
5. **3 courgettes (all)** + **2 tins chickpeas (all)**, 15 min more until the lentils are soft. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick; lentils keep drinking.
6. **The moroccan lemon juice (2 lemons' worth)**, **all the coriander**, season hard. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Method — Side: Lemony Braised Savoy Cabbage, Leek & Peas (~24 side portions, two batches)

*Shared: garlic, lemons, olive oil. Exclusive: all the cabbage, leeks, peas, stock. Cooked as two identical batches, one in rinsed Pot C and one in rinsed Pot B. Each batch takes half of everything below.*

Per batch:
1. **4 tbsp olive oil (half of the remaining 8)**, **6 leeks (½ of the 12)** + pinch of salt, 8 min until soft, not coloured.
2. **8 sliced garlic (8 of the 25 sliced; the other 8 go in batch 2)**, 1 min.
3. **2 cabbages' worth of shreds (½ of the pile)** + **400 ml stock (½ of the 800 ml)** + **half the side lemon zest**. Lid on, braise 15 min, stirring now and then, until silky.
4. **1 kg frozen peas (½ of the 2 kg)**, 5 min.
5. **Half the side lemon juice**, half the herbs if using, lots of pepper, season.
6. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Freeze sequencing

- **Everything freezes.** Keep ~3 days in the fridge (1 of each main + side), freeze the rest.
- **Pairings:** the side goes with all three. There are 27 mains and 24 sides, so 3 meals go without a side. Make those the stifado, which already has spinach in it; add feta.
- **Eat order:** no fish this cook, so there's no rush. The stifado improves after a few days, so eat it last.
- Label containers. The jerk pork and the stifado look alike frozen.
- **All four recipes are untested.** Taste each pot before portioning and fix the seasoning then, while you still can.
