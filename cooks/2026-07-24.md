# Cook — 2026-07-24

**Recipes:** Pork & White Bean Stew (2×), Thai Red Curry Chicken (2×)
**Yield:** ~24 portions total (~12 each). ~12 days food.

## Shopping list

### Produce
- 4 onions
- 12 garlic cloves
- 2 thumbs ginger
- 400 g kale
- 4 bell peppers
- 400 g green beans
- 600 g spinach
- 2 limes

### Meat / fish
- 2.4 kg pork shoulder, cubed
- 2.4 kg boneless skinless chicken thigh, diced
- (optional) 200 g chorizo

### Pantry
- 4 × 400 g tin cannellini (white) beans
- 4 × 400 g tin chickpeas
- 4 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 2 × 400 ml light coconut milk
- 8 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 4 tbsp fish sauce
- 2 tbsp smoked paprika
- oil, bay, salt, pepper, ~1000 ml stock

## Mise en place
- Cube pork (2.4 kg); dice chicken thigh (2.4 kg) — keep separate.
- Dice 4 onions; mince 12 garlic cloves (split: 8 pork / 4 thai); grate 2 thumbs ginger.
- Stem + chop 400 g kale; chop 600 g spinach.
- Slice 4 bell peppers; trim 400 g green beans.
- Drain: 4 tins cannellini, 4 tins chickpeas.
- Halve 2 limes.

## Cook plan (single session, two pots)
1. **Pork pot first** (longest braise). Brown pork in batches; soften onions + pork garlic; add paprika, tomatoes, stock, bay. Set to low simmer 1.5–2 hr.
2. While pork simmers, **start Thai pot.** Fry thai garlic + ginger + curry paste; brown chicken; add coconut milk + chickpeas + peppers + green beans + water. Simmer 25–30 min.
3. Finish Thai: wilt spinach, add fish sauce + lime. Cool.
4. Back to pork: stir in beans + kale, 15 min to thicken. Season. Cool.
5. Portion + freeze both.

## Method — Pork & White Bean Stew (2×)
1. Brown 2.4 kg pork in batches (don't crowd), set aside. If using chorizo (200 g), render first + reserve oil.
2. Soften 4 onions + 8 garlic cloves in fat until gold; stir in 2 tbsp smoked paprika 30 sec.
3. Return pork, add 4 × 400 g tomatoes + ~1000 ml stock + bay. Simmer low 1.5–2 hr until tender.
4. Stir in 4 × 400 g cannellini + 400 g kale, simmer 15 min to wilt + thicken. Season.
5. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Method — Thai Red Curry Chicken (2×)
1. Soften 4 garlic cloves + 2 thumbs ginger in 2 tbsp oil. Add 8 tbsp red curry paste, fry 1 min.
2. Add 2.4 kg chicken thigh, brown.
3. Add 2 × 400 ml coconut milk + 4 × 400 g chickpeas + 4 peppers + 400 g green beans + ~400 ml water. Simmer 25–30 min.
4. Stir through 600 g spinach to wilt, add 4 tbsp fish sauce + juice of 2 limes. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Freeze sequencing
- Both freeze excellently (~3 months). No fresh fish this cook → no rush portions.
- Eat a few pork + thai fresh from fridge days 1–3; freeze the rest.
