# Chicken Paprikash

**Batch:** ~6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent — but freeze the base **without** the sour cream (dairy splits). **Cuisine:** Hungarian. **Ed's own recipe.**

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~480 kcal · Protein 40g · Fat 28g · Carb 14g · Fiber 4g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (~6 portions)
- 1.4 kg chicken thigh, boneless skinless, halved
- 3 onions, finely diced
- 3 red bell peppers, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 4 tbsp sweet Hungarian paprika (the volume is the point — don't cut it)
- 1 tsp hot paprika or cayenne
- 2 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 300 ml full-fat sour cream or crème fraîche — **stirred in at serving, not before freezing**
- (cupboard) oil or lard, salt, pepper, 400 ml chicken stock

## Method
1. Brown chicken in batches in oil or lard, set aside.
2. Soften onions + peppers slowly in the fat, 10–12 min, until properly sweet. Add garlic 1 min.
3. **Off the heat**, stir in both paprikas — paprika burns bitter over direct flame. 30 sec in the residual heat.
4. Back on low heat: tomatoes + stock + chicken. Simmer 40–50 min until the sauce is thick and deep red.
5. Season. **Split here:** for the portions you're eating now, stir sour cream into the warm (not boiling) pot. Freeze the rest plain.
6. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen, or defrost overnight + 3 min. **Then** stir in ~50 ml sour cream per portion off the heat. Boiling after the cream goes in will curdle it.
