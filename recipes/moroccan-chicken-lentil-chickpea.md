# Moroccan Chicken, Lentil & Chickpea Stew

**Batch:** 6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months — flavour deepens). **Cuisine:** Moroccan (tagine-style — harissa, cumin, cinnamon, lemon). One pot.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~490 kcal · Protein 50g · Fat 14g · Carb 38g · Fiber 13g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 portions)
- 1.2 kg boneless skinless chicken thigh, bite-size chunks
- 200 g green or brown lentils (dry), rinsed
- 1 × 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
- 2 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 4 carrots, thick coins
- 2 courgettes, chunked
- 2 onions, diced
- 6 garlic cloves, minced + 20 g ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp harissa (AH, jar)
- 2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp ground coriander, 1 tsp cinnamon, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 lemon — zest + juice
- 1 bunch fresh coriander, chopped
- (cupboard) 2 tbsp olive oil, salt, ~900 ml water or stock

## Method
1. Oil hot. Brown chicken in batches, set aside.
2. Onions 8 min until soft. Garlic, ginger, all dry spices 1 min. Harissa 30 sec.
3. Tomatoes, lentils, carrots, water + lemon zest. Chicken back in. Lid on, simmer 25 min.
4. Courgettes + chickpeas in, 15 min more until lentils are soft. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick — lentils keep drinking.
5. Lemon juice, coriander, season hard. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen, splash of water. Spoon of yoghurt on top at serving.
