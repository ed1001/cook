# Filipino Chicken Adobo

**Batch:** ~6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months — improves with age). **Cuisine:** Filipino (soy, vinegar, garlic, bay). **Ed's own recipe.**

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~450 kcal · Protein 41g · Fat 26g · Carb 8g · Fiber 2g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (~6 portions)
- 1.6 kg chicken thigh, bone-in skin-on (or 1.3 kg boneless thigh, or 1.6 kg **bouten** — split at the joint, simmer 45–55 min, yields ~5 portions as there's more bone)
- 150 ml soy sauce
- 150 ml cane or white wine vinegar
- 1 whole head garlic (~12 cloves), crushed
- 2 onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp whole black peppercorns, cracked
- 6 bay leaves
- 400 g green beans or long beans, trimmed (added at the end, keeps it a full meal)
- (cupboard) oil, 200 ml water

## Method
1. Marinate chicken in soy, vinegar, garlic, peppercorns + bay — 30 min minimum, ideally overnight in the fridge.
2. Lift chicken out (keep the marinade). Sear skin-side down in a hot dry pot until browned, in batches. Set aside.
3. Soften onions in the rendered fat.
4. Return chicken + all the marinade + water. Simmer uncovered 35–45 min, turning once, until the sauce is glossy and reduced by half. Don't stir the vinegar off too early — let it cook out for the first 10 min untouched.
5. Add beans for the last 6–8 min.
6. Taste — it should be sharp and salty, not sweet. Cool, portion (bone-in portions keep best), freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 5 min from frozen, or defrost overnight + 3 min. Sauce thickens in the fridge; a splash of water loosens it.
