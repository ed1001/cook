# Chicken Saag & Chickpeas

**Batch:** 6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months). **Cuisine:** Indian (Punjabi saag — spinach-heavy, ginger, garam masala). Frozen spinach keeps it cheap and bulk-friendly.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~510 kcal · Protein 54g · Fat 16g · Carb 30g · Fiber 12g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 portions)
- 1.2 kg boneless skinless chicken thigh, bite-size chunks
- 150 g full-fat Greek yoghurt (marinade only — not stirred in, so nothing splits in the freezer)
- 1 kg frozen chopped spinach (AH freezer, blocks)
- 2 × 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
- 1 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 3 onions, finely diced
- 8 garlic cloves + 50 g ginger, grated
- 2 green chillies, slit
- 2 tbsp garam masala, 2 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 lemon, juiced
- (cupboard) 2 tbsp oil or ghee, salt, ~300 ml water

## Method
1. Marinate chicken in yoghurt + 1 tbsp of the garam masala + turmeric + salt, 30 min or overnight.
2. Oil hot, cumin seeds 30 sec until they crackle. Onions low and slow 12–15 min until deep gold — this is the flavour base, don't rush it.
3. Garlic, ginger, chillies 2 min. Ground coriander + rest of the garam masala 30 sec. Tomatoes, cook down 5 min.
4. Chicken in with all its marinade, stir 5 min until it turns opaque. Add spinach straight from frozen + water. Lid on, simmer 25–30 min, stirring as the blocks break down.
5. Chickpeas in, lid off 5–10 min to thicken. Lemon, season hard.
6. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen. Spoon of yoghurt on top at serving.
