# Korean Gochujang Beef & Bean Stew

**Batch:** 6 portions. **Freezes:** perfect. **Cuisine:** Korean.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~480 kcal · Protein 44g · Fat 15g · Carb 44g · Fiber 13g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 portions)
- 1 kg lean beef mince (5% fat)
- 3 tbsp gochujang (chili paste — has some sugar, use as measured; total still low-GI)
- 1 tbsp gochugaru (chili flakes) to taste
- 2 × 400 g black beans (drained) — slow carb + fiber
- 1 × 400 g chopped tomatoes
- 250 g mushrooms, 2 onions, 6 garlic cloves, thumb of ginger
- 4 spring onions, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp soy/tamari

## Method
1. Brown mince, set aside. Soften onion/garlic/ginger/mushrooms in sesame oil.
2. Stir in gochujang + gochugaru, cook 1 min. Return mince.
3. Add tomatoes + black beans + ~200 ml water + soy. Simmer 30 min.
4. Stir through spring onions. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen. Kimchi on top at serving (natural pairing).
