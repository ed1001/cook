# Bulgogi Beef & Cabbage

**Batch:** 6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months). **Cuisine:** Korean (bulgogi flavours — garlic, soy, sesame, grated pear — on mince). Mild, savoury-sweet; no sugar, the pear does the sweetening. Cabbage keeps it cheap and bulky.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~475 kcal · Protein 52g · Fat 20g · Carb 18g · Fiber 9g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 portions)
- 1.2 kg lean beef mince (5% fat)
- ½ large white cabbage (~800 g), cored + shredded
- 3 carrots, matchsticks
- 2 onions, sliced
- 400 g frozen shelled edamame (AH freezer)
- 1 ripe pear (Conference or nashi), grated
- 6 tbsp soy/tamari
- 8 garlic cloves + ~30 g ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted
- 6 spring onions, sliced
- (cupboard) oil, lots of black pepper, ~200 ml water; gochugaru optional at serving

## Method
1. Sauce in a jug: grate the pear straight into the soy (stops it browning), add garlic, ginger, sesame oil + lots of black pepper.
2. Brown mince hard in batches — colour, not grey. Set aside.
3. Splash of oil, onions + carrots 5 min. Mince back, all the sauce, bubble 2 min.
4. Cabbage in by the handful + water. Lid on, 15–20 min until soft but with a bit of bite — it softens more on reheat.
5. Edamame straight from frozen, 5–10 min. Stir through spring onions + sesame seeds. Taste — flat → more soy.
6. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen. Pinch of gochugaru or a spoon of kimchi on top if you want heat.
