# Side — Lemony Braised Savoy Cabbage, Leek & Peas

**Batch:** 6 side portions. **Side:** freezer. **Freezes:** good (holds ~3 months — braised, so no texture loss). **Cuisine:** neutral/Med — a clean green, garlic and lemon, built to sit next to any rich spiced stew (jerk pork, Moroccan chicken, stifado).

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~140 kcal · Protein 6g · Fat 5g · Carb 16g · Fiber 7g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 side portions)
- 1 savoy cabbage (~900 g), cored, shredded ~1 cm
- 3 leeks, halved lengthways, washed, sliced
- 500 g frozen peas
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 lemon — zest + juice
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 200 ml chicken or veg stock
- (cupboard) salt, lots of black pepper, chilli flakes optional; handful parsley or dill optional

## Method
1. Oil in a wide pot. Leeks + pinch of salt, 8 min until soft, not coloured. Garlic 1 min.
2. Cabbage + stock + lemon zest. Lid on, braise 15 min, stirring now and then, until silky.
3. Peas straight from frozen, 5 min.
4. Lemon juice, herbs, pepper, season. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 3–4 min from frozen. Fresh squeeze of lemon if it tastes flat.
