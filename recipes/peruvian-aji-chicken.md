# Peruvian-style Yellow Chili Chicken & Bean Stew

**Batch:** 6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent. **Cuisine:** Peruvian-ish. **Hot.**

_Was an ají amarillo recipe. Ají amarillo isn't sold anywhere near Buitenveldert, so this is the madame jeanette build — roasted yellow bell pepper for the fruity body ají brings, madame jeanette for heat. Filename kept as `peruvian-aji-chicken` so old cook plans still link._

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~490 kcal · Protein 47g · Fat 17g · Carb 44g · Fiber 11g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 portions)
- 1.2 kg boneless skinless chicken thigh, diced
- 2 yellow or orange bell peppers — roasted, peeled, blended (the sauce body)
- **1 madame jeanette**, deseeded, membrane kept, minced fine — see heat dial
- ½ tsp turmeric (colour)
- 2 × 400 g butter beans / cannellini (drained) — slow carb + fiber
- 1 × 400 g chopped tomatoes
- 2 onions, 6 garlic cloves
- 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp oregano
- 1 red bell pepper, handful coriander, juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp oil

## Heat dial (per 6 portions)
| Peppers | Result |
|---|---|
| ½, deseeded | Warm. Background heat. |
| **1, deseeded, membrane in** | **Quite hot. Sweat on the forehead, still eatable daily.** ← this recipe |
| 1 whole, seeds + membrane in | Hot. Noticeably punchy. |
| 2+ | Brutal. Don't do this to 12 frozen portions. |

Madame jeanette runs ~100–350k SHU and varies a lot pepper to pepper, so the same recipe lands differently each batch. Keep the membrane (that's where the capsaicin is), bin the seeds (bitterness, little heat).

**Handling:** wash hands with soap and water after — plain water spreads it. Don't rub eyes for a couple of hours. Heat does not cook off during the simmer and does not fade in the freezer, so an over-hot batch stays over-hot for all 6 portions.

## Method
1. Roast the 2 yellow/orange peppers at 220°C for ~25 min until blistered. Cool, peel, blend smooth with the turmeric. _(Do this first — they need to cool.)_
2. Soften onion + garlic + red pepper in oil.
3. Add the roasted-pepper blend + minced madame jeanette + cumin + oregano. Cook 2–3 min to drive off the water and take the raw edge off.
4. Add chicken, brown.
5. Add tomatoes + beans + ~300 ml water. Simmer 30–35 min until sauce thickens.
6. Stir through coriander + lime. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen, or defrost overnight + 3 min. Fresh coriander + fermented side at serving — yoghurt or kefir also takes the edge off if a batch came out hotter than planned.
