# Peruvian Ají Chicken & Bean Stew

**Batch:** 6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent. **Cuisine:** Peruvian.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~490 kcal · Protein 47g · Fat 17g · Carb 42g · Fiber 11g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 portions)
- 1.2 kg boneless skinless chicken thigh, diced
- 3 tbsp ají amarillo paste (Peruvian yellow chili)
- 2 × 400 g butter beans / cannellini (drained) — slow carb + fiber
- 1 × 400 g chopped tomatoes
- 2 onions, 6 garlic cloves
- 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp oregano
- 1 red bell pepper, handful coriander, juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp oil

## Method
1. Soften onion/garlic/pepper in oil. Add ají paste + cumin + oregano, toast 1 min.
2. Add chicken, brown.
3. Add tomatoes + beans + ~300 ml water. Simmer 30–35 min until sauce thickens.
4. Stir through coriander + lime. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen, or defrost overnight + 3 min. Fresh coriander + fermented side at serving.
