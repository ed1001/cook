# Pork & White Bean Stew

**Batch:** ~6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months). **Cuisine:** Spanish-ish (pork, paprika, beans). **Ed's own recipe.**

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~460 kcal · Protein 38g · Fat 22g · Carb 24g · Fiber 8g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (~6 portions)
- 1.2 kg pork shoulder, cubed
- 2 × 400 g tin cannellini (white) beans, drained
- 2 onions, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 200 g kale, stemmed + roughly chopped
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- (optional) 100 g chorizo, diced — adds depth + fat
- (cupboard) oil, bay, salt, pepper, 500 ml stock or water

## Method
1. Brown pork in batches (don't crowd), set aside. If using chorizo, render it first + reserve the oil.
2. Soften onions + garlic in the fat until gold; stir in paprika 30 sec.
3. Return pork, add tomatoes + stock + bay. Simmer low 1.5–2 hr until pork tender.
4. Stir in beans + kale, simmer 15 min to wilt + thicken. Season.
5. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen, or defrost overnight + 3 min. Splash of water if thick; simmer 1 min to loosen.
