# Mexican Pork Verde (Chile Verde)

**Batch:** ~6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months). **Cuisine:** Mexican (green chile, tomatillo, lime). **Tomatillos aren't sold locally — see the substitution in Ingredients.** **Ed's own recipe.**

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~470 kcal · Protein 40g · Fat 23g · Carb 20g · Fiber 7g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (~6 portions)
- 1.2 kg pork shoulder, cubed
- 4 green bell peppers, quartered + deseeded
- 2 × 400 g tin tomatillos **— not available in Buitenveldert.** Substitute: 2 extra green bell peppers (6 total) + 50 ml white wine vinegar, added at the blitz
- 2 jalapeños, stemmed (leave seeds for heat)
- 2 onions, quartered
- 6 garlic cloves, peeled
- 1 × 400 g tin black beans, drained
- 1 tbsp ground cumin
- 1 big bunch coriander, stalks + leaves
- 2 limes, juiced
- (cupboard) oil, dried oregano, salt, pepper, 400 ml stock or water

## Method
1. Oven 220°C. Roast peppers, jalapeños, onion + garlic on a tray 25–30 min until blackening at the edges.
2. Meanwhile brown pork in batches in a heavy pot (don't crowd), set aside.
3. Blitz the roasted veg with tomatillos, coriander stalks + cumin to a coarse green sauce. **Without tomatillos:** blitz with the coriander stalks, cumin and 50 ml vinegar — the sour comes from vinegar + lime instead, and the extra peppers carry the body. Drop the stock to 300 ml since there's no tin liquid.
4. Return pork to the pot with the sauce + stock + oregano. Simmer low 1.5–2 hr until pork shreds under a fork.
5. Stir in black beans, lime juice + chopped coriander leaves. Simmer 10 min. Season hard — it needs salt.
6. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen, or defrost overnight + 3 min. Fresh lime over the top wakes it up.
