# Thai Red Curry Chicken

**Batch:** 6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent (flavor deepens). **Cuisine:** Thai / SE Asian.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~500 kcal · Protein 48g · Fat 20g · Carb 38g · Fiber 10g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 portions)
- 1.2 kg boneless skinless chicken thigh, diced
- 4 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 1 × 400 ml light coconut milk
- 2 × 400 g chickpeas (drained) — slow carb + fiber + protein
- 2 bell peppers, 200 g green beans, 300 g spinach
- 2 garlic cloves, thumb of ginger
- 2 tbsp fish sauce, juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp oil

## Method
1. Soften garlic/ginger in oil. Add curry paste, fry 1 min until fragrant.
2. Add chicken, brown.
3. Add coconut milk + chickpeas + peppers + green beans + ~200 ml water. Simmer 25–30 min.
4. Stir through spinach to wilt, add fish sauce + lime. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen, or defrost overnight + 3 min. Fresh lime + fermented side at serving.
