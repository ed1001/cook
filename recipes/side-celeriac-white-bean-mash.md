# Side — Celeriac & White Bean Mash

**Batch:** 6 side portions. **Side:** freezer. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months). **Cuisine:** neutral — replaces rice/potato under any stew.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~250 kcal · Protein 9g · Fat 8g · Carb 30g · Fiber 9g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 side portions)
- 2 celeriac (~1.6 kg whole, ~1.2 kg peeled), cubed
- 2 × 400 g tin cannellini beans, drained
- 6 garlic cloves, peeled whole
- 45 ml olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- (cupboard) nutmeg, salt, pepper

## Method
1. Simmer celeriac + whole garlic in salted water ~20 min until very soft. Add beans for the last 3 min to warm.
2. Drain well, let steam-dry 5 min (wet mash freezes icy).
3. Blitz or mash with olive oil, lemon, nutmeg, salt + pepper. Should be thick — it loosens on reheat.
4. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 3–4 min from frozen, stir halfway. Splash of water if stiff.
