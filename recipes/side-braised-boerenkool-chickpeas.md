# Side — Braised Boerenkool, Leek & Chickpeas

**Batch:** 6 side portions. **Side:** freezer. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months). **Cuisine:** neutral (garlic + leek only) — sits under any main without clashing. Bagged boerenkool is cheap and bulk-friendly — stands in for cavolo nero.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~220 kcal · Protein 11g · Fat 7g · Carb 26g · Fiber 11g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 side portions)
- 600 g chopped boerenkool (2 × 300 g bags, AH)
- 3 leeks, washed, sliced
- 2 × 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
- 6 garlic cloves, sliced
- 30 ml olive oil
- (cupboard) salt, pepper, ~200 ml stock or water

## Method
1. Soften leeks in olive oil with a pinch of salt, 8–10 min. Add garlic, 1 min.
2. Add boerenkool in handfuls with the stock, lid on, braise 15–20 min until tender (boerenkool is tougher than cavolo nero — don't rush it).
3. Add chickpeas, cook uncovered 5 min to drive off liquid. Season.
4. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 3–4 min from frozen.
