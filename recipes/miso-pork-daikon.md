# Miso-Ginger Pork Shoulder & Daikon

**Batch:** 6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months). **Cuisine:** Japanese (buta-no-kakuni-ish braise, rebuilt without sugar or mirin).

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~520 kcal · Protein 40g · Fat 28g · Carb 18g · Fiber 7g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 portions)
- 1.2 kg pork shoulder, cubed ~4 cm, visible fat trimmed
- 4 tbsp miso (white or red — Shilla)
- 600 g daikon / Korean radish (mu), peeled, thick half-moons (Shilla)
- 300 g shiitake or chestnut mushrooms, halved
- 3 carrots, chunky
- 1 × 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
- 8 garlic cloves, crushed
- big thumb of ginger (~50 g), half sliced, half grated
- 4 tbsp soy/tamari, 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 6 spring onions, sliced
- (cupboard) oil, ~800 ml water or stock

## Method
1. Brown pork in batches (don't crowd), set aside.
2. Fry garlic + sliced ginger 1 min. Return pork, add soy + water/stock to just cover. Simmer low, lid ajar, 1 hr.
3. Add daikon, carrots + mushrooms. Simmer 45 min–1 hr until pork is spoon-tender and daikon translucent.
4. Loosen miso with a ladle of the liquid, stir in **off the boil** (boiling kills the flavour). Add chickpeas, grated ginger + rice vinegar, warm 5 min. Stir through spring onions.
5. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen. Chilli oil or shichimi on top if you have it.
