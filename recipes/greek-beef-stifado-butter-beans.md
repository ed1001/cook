# Greek Beef Stifado with Butter Beans & Spinach

**Batch:** 6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months — better on day 2). **Cuisine:** Greek (stifado — sweet-sour onion and tomato braise, cinnamon, clove, red wine vinegar). One pot.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~490 kcal · Protein 46g · Fat 18g · Carb 30g · Fiber 9g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 portions)
- 1.2 kg braising beef (stoofvlees / chuck), cubed ~4 cm (T-Hakblok)
- 2 × 400 g tin butter beans, drained
- 400 g frozen chopped spinach (AH freezer)
- 750 g small onions or shallots, peeled whole (or 4 onions, thick wedges)
- 2 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 6 garlic cloves, sliced
- 100 ml red wine vinegar
- 150 ml red wine (optional — use water if skipping)
- 1 cinnamon stick, 4 cloves, 3 bay leaves, 1 tsp ground allspice, 2 tsp dried oregano
- (cupboard) 2 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper, ~300 ml water

## Method
1. Oil hot. Brown beef hard in batches, set aside.
2. Onions whole into the fat, 10 min until browned in patches. Garlic + tomato purée 2 min.
3. Vinegar + wine, scrape the bottom, bubble 2 min. Tomatoes, water, all spices + oregano. Beef back in.
4. Lid on, simmer very low 2 hr until the beef pulls apart. (Or 160 °C oven, same time.)
5. Beans + spinach straight from frozen. Lid off 15 min, stirring as the spinach breaks down, until thick.
6. Fish out cinnamon, cloves + bay. Season. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen. Crumble of feta at serving.
