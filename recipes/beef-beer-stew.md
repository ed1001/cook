# Beef & Dark Beer Stew

**Batch:** ~7 portions. **Freezes:** excellent (flavor deepens). **Cuisine:** Flemish-ish (carbonnade). **Ed's own recipe.**

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~450 kcal · Protein 42g · Fat 20g · Carb 18g · Fiber 4g · Low-GI

_No legume/grain in this one → lower carb than the other cores. Add a carb side (rice/potato) at assembly to hit daily target._

## Ingredients (~7 portions)
- 1.5 kg stewing beef, cubed
- 1 × 330–500 ml dark beer (dubbel / stout / bruin)
- 3 onions, sliced
- 4 carrots, chunked
- (cupboard) oil, bay, salt, pepper — optional: 1 tbsp mustard, thyme, splash vinegar to balance

## Method
1. Brown beef in batches (don't crowd), set aside.
2. Soften onions slowly in the fat until deep gold.
3. Return beef, add beer + carrots + bay + water to nearly cover.
4. Simmer low 2–2.5 hr until beef pull-apart tender. Reduce to thicken.
5. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen, or defrost overnight + 3 min. Add a splash of water if it has thickened; simmer 1 min to loosen.
