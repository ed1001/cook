# Side — Roast Ratatouille

**Batch:** 6 side portions. **Side:** freezer. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months). **Cuisine:** French. Roasting instead of stewing keeps it from going watery in the freezer.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~185 kcal · Protein 5g · Fat 8g · Carb 20g · Fiber 8g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 side portions)
- 2 aubergines, 2–3 cm cubes
- 3 courgettes, 2–3 cm cubes
- 3 red bell peppers, chunks
- 2 onions, wedges
- 6 garlic cloves, smashed
- 2 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 45 ml olive oil
- (cupboard) herbes de Provence or dried thyme, salt, pepper

## Method
1. Oven 220°C. Toss aubergine, courgette, peppers, onion + garlic with olive oil, herbs, salt. Spread over 2 trays (crowded = steamed).
2. Roast 35–40 min, turning once, until browned at the edges.
3. Meanwhile simmer tomatoes 15 min to thicken. Fold in roast veg, season.
4. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 3–4 min from frozen.
