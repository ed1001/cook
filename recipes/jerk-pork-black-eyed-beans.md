# Jerk Pork, Black-Eyed Beans & Squash

**Batch:** 6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months). **Cuisine:** Caribbean (jerk — allspice, thyme, scotch-bonnet heat via madame jeanette, lime). No brown sugar; the squash does the sweetening. One pot.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~520 kcal · Protein 42g · Fat 22g · Carb 34g · Fiber 10g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 portions)
- 1.2 kg pork shoulder, cubed ~4 cm, visible fat trimmed (T-Hakblok)
- 2 × 400 g tin black-eyed beans, drained
- 600 g butternut squash, peeled, 3 cm chunks (~1 small squash)
- 2 red bell peppers, chunked
- 2 onions, diced
- 6 spring onions — whites sliced into the paste, greens kept for the end
- 6 garlic cloves + 30 g ginger
- 1–2 madame jeanette peppers (AH, 100 g pack), deseeded for less heat — wear gloves
- 1 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 2 limes, juiced
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp ground allspice, 1 tbsp dried thyme (or a big bunch fresh), 1 tsp cinnamon, 1 tsp black pepper
- (cupboard) 2 tbsp oil, salt, ~400 ml water

## Method
1. Jerk paste: blitz spring onion whites, garlic, ginger, madame jeanette, half the lime juice, soy, allspice, thyme, cinnamon, pepper. Toss pork in it — 30 min, or overnight in the fridge is better.
2. Oil hot in a big pot. Brown pork in batches, scraping the paste off the pan bottom as you go; set aside.
3. Onions + peppers into the fat, 8 min until soft.
4. Pork + any leftover paste back in. Tomatoes + water. Lid on, simmer low 1 hr.
5. Squash + beans in. Lid on 25–30 min until pork is tender and squash is soft but holds shape.
6. Rest of the lime, spring onion greens, season. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen, stir halfway. Extra squeeze of lime at serving.
