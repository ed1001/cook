# Side — Braised Red Cabbage & Apple

**Batch:** 6 side portions. **Side:** freezer. **Freezes:** excellent (holds ~3 months — gets better). **Cuisine:** Dutch/German (rodekool), no added sugar — sweetness from apple only.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~155 kcal · Protein 4g · Fat 4g · Carb 22g · Fiber 6g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 side portions)
- 1 large red cabbage (~1.5 kg), cored, finely sliced
- 2 tart apples (e.g. Elstar, Goudreinet), grated
- 2 onions, sliced
- 100 ml red wine or cider vinegar
- 1 cinnamon stick, 3 cloves, 2 bay leaves
- 30 g butter or 2 tbsp oil
- (cupboard) salt, pepper, ~150 ml water

## Method
1. Soften onions in butter 5 min.
2. Add cabbage, apple, vinegar, spices, water + good pinch of salt. Lid on, braise low 1 hr, stirring now and then — should be glossy and soft, not wet. Lid off at the end if there's liquid left.
3. Fish out cinnamon, cloves + bay. Season.
4. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 3–4 min from frozen.
