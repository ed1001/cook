# Salmon Fishcakes

**Batch:** 8 cakes = 4 portions (2 cakes each). **Freezes:** clean (unlike traybake). **Cuisine:** Med-ish. **Eat early week** (omega-3, freshest first).

## Per-portion macros (approx, 2 cakes)
- ~380 kcal · Protein 34g · Fat 18g (omega-3) · Carb 28g · Fiber 5g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (8 cakes)
- 600 g salmon fillet, skinned
- 500 g sweet potato (lower-GI binder), cooked + mashed
- 2 eggs (bind)
- 4 spring onions, handful parsley/dill, zest of 1 lemon
- 50 g chickpea flour / kikkererwtenmeel (low-GI coating, not breadcrumb)
- Salt, pepper

## Method
1. Poach or bake salmon 12 min, flake.
2. Mix salmon + mashed sweet potato + egg + onion + herbs + lemon. Form 8 cakes.
3. Roll in chickpea flour.
4. Pan-fry 3 min/side to set, OR oven-bake 200°C 20 min. Cool, freeze on a tray then bag.

## Reheat
Oven 180°C 15 min from frozen (keeps texture). Serve with big salad + fermented side.
