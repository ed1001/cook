# Beef Chili

**Batch:** 6 portions. **Freezes:** perfect. **Cuisine:** Mexican/Latin.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~470 kcal · Protein 42g · Fat 14g · Carb 45g · Fiber 14g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 portions)
- 1 kg lean beef mince (5% fat)
- 2 × 400 g black beans (drained), 1 × 400 g kidney beans (drained) — fiber + slow carb
- 2 × 400 g chopped tomatoes
- 2 bell peppers, 2 onions, 4 garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp oil
- Spices: 1 tbsp each cumin + smoked paprika; 1 tsp oregano; chili to taste

## Method
1. Brown mince, set aside. Soften onion/garlic/peppers in oil.
2. Add spices, toast 1 min. Return mince.
3. Add tomatoes + beans. Simmer 30 min. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen. Spoon kimchi/sauerkraut on top at serving.
