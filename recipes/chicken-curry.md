# Indian Chicken Thigh Curry

**Batch:** 6 portions. **Freezes:** excellent (flavor deepens). **Cuisine:** Indian.

## Per-portion macros (approx)
- ~500 kcal · Protein 52g · Fat 18g · Carb 42g · Fiber 10g · Low-GI

## Ingredients (6 portions)
- 1.2 kg boneless skinless chicken thigh, diced
- 300 g red lentils (dry) — protein + fiber + slow carb
- 2 onions, 6 garlic cloves, thumb of ginger
- 2 × 400 g chopped tomatoes
- 400 g spinach
- 2 tbsp oil
- Spices: 2 tsp each cumin, ground coriander, turmeric; 1 tbsp garam masala; chili to taste

## Method
1. Soften onion/garlic/ginger in oil. Add spices, toast 1 min.
2. Add chicken, brown.
3. Add tomatoes + lentils + 500 ml water. Simmer 30–40 min until lentils soft.
4. Stir through spinach to wilt. Cool, portion, freeze.

## Reheat
Microwave 4–5 min from frozen, or defrost overnight + 3 min. Add fermented side at serving.
